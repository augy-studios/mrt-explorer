      About this map

      Singapore's MRT and LRT network on one map. Tap a station for its codes and its name in English, Chinese and Tamil, or search by any of them.

      Once it has loaded, the lines, stations and search work without a connection. The street map underneath shows offline for the areas you have already viewed.

      • Line paths are simplified and smoothed. They show which stations a line connects, not where the real track runs.
      • This is a reference map. It does not plan journeys or show arrival times.

      Station and line data from sgraildata by Lim Chee Aun, compiled from LTA DataMall, Wikipedia and OpenStreetMap contributors. Snapshot of 12 July 2026. Street map tiles from OpenStreetMap, drawn with Leaflet.
